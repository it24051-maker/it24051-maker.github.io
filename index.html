// 01 — about

About me

I'm an undergraduate student in the Department of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) at Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University, Bangladesh. ICT appealed to me because it sits between software and hardware — I get to write code and also wire up the circuits that code runs on.

My work so far spans Java-based web applications, microcontroller projects with Arduino and ESP32, and problem solving through competitive programming. I enjoy projects with a practical, local angle — like tools built around Bangladeshi geography, farming, or campus life — rather than purely abstract exercises.

Outside of coursework, I spend time on LaTeX-typeset technical reports, IoT prototyping in Wokwi, and sharpening my data structures & algorithms fundamentals.
